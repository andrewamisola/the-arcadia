About

Video Games & Interactive Media

The Arcadia is a media platform for video games and interactive culture — built on the belief that games are an authentic art form deserving of serious attention.

A Different Lane

Film has art house cinema. Music has independent labels. Literature has small presses that care about craft over commerce. Video games deserve the same — a space where the medium is treated with the weight it has earned, not reduced to product cycles and hype.

We cover games, digital art, and the culture surrounding them. Not as entertainment news, but as culture worth examining.

For Those Who Play Differently

There's a growing audience of people who play games seriously but don't identify with the traditional "gamer" label. Professionals, creatives, people with lives outside their hobby — who appreciate interactive media the way others appreciate film or literature.

The Arcadia exists for them. No stigma. No subculture gatekeeping. Just the work, treated with respect.

Submissions

We publish clips and moments from our community. If you capture something worth sharing — a perfect play, a rare glitch, a beautiful frame — you can submit it through our Discord bot.

We handle the editing, captioning, and presentation. You maintain ownership of your work, get credited however you choose, and can be rewarded for standout submissions. Your content lives on our channels, but it's still yours.

What We're Building

The Arcadia is more than a publication. It's a platform — starting with submissions, expanding into editorial coverage, community tools, and more. The bot is just the beginning.

We're building a home for interactive media that doesn't exist yet. A place where games are treated like the art form they've always been.
